### ⭐ 2b · THE MISSING LAYERS ARE NAMED, NOT SILENTLY DROPPED (Neil, ruled 2026-08-24)

*"they would be missing a lot of stuff from the later units — how to build empathy, theme and
tone and genre and structural elements… we need to sort of help to remind them about those."*
Stopping at Trial 3 means four layers of the full project are never met. Two mechanisms, ruled:

1. ⭐ **Trial 3's priority names the ONE layer THIS student's story would gain most from** —
   empathy, theme and tone, genre, or structural elements. Their own draft, at the moment they
   care, chosen for them. This is the load-bearing half: a generic list teaches nothing, a
   pointed sentence about their own story does.
2. **A short closing lesson (unit lesson 14, if built) shows all four with ONE worked example
   each** — so they know what is waiting rather than not knowing it exists.

⛔ A checklist alone was proposed and **rejected** as teaching-by-listing. §27 (examples on demand
are generous) and §4c.2 both apply: the closing lesson's four examples are PULL, not push.

⚠️ **Trials 2 and 3 are not yet rebuilt.** Only Trial 1 has the new architecture (84 lines,
code-served, two-level self-marking ladder, 2026-08-23). Trials 2–6 are 22–23 lines each in the
superseded *"score each criterion 1–5"* format. **The unit cannot ship its lessons 10 and 13
until the engine lane's trial slice covers them.**
