# Mark Scheme Assessment Bank — Leave Taking

Deterministic, code-scored MARK-SCHEME-LITERACY assessment bank (parsed by `SWML_Quiz_Bank::pick_session_msa`).
Examiner-level — harder + more precise than the Mark Scheme Quiz drill. Every question makes the
student JUDGE real writing about Leave Taking against the EXACT mark-scheme descriptor wording, and forces
them to USE the mark scheme's own vocabulary rather than a simplified paraphrase. Keys + feedback live
server-side and are stripped before questions reach the client. The AI is never the scorekeeper.

AOs tagged per question for stratified random selection (the picker spreads the 10 across each board's own AO split).
Question types: MCQ · Select All · Fill · Ranking. Boards differ: AQA (8702 Modern Texts) = AO1+AO2+AO3;
Edexcel GCSE (1ET0 Post-1914) = AO1+AO3+AO4(SPaG), AO2 NOT assessed; Eduqas (Post-1914 Prose/Drama) = AO1+AO2+AO4(SPaG), AO3 NOT assessed;
OCR (J352 Modern Prose/Drama) = AO1+AO2+AO3+AO4; Cambridge IGCSE (0475 Drama) = AO1+AO2+AO3+AO4 holistic, AO4 = Personal Response (not SPaG).

### **SECTION A: AQA (8702 — Modern Texts)**

1. **Type: MCQ [Tests AO1]**
   * **Question:** A student writes: "In Leave Taking, Enid comes to England from Jamaica, works hard cleaning offices, and raises her two daughters Del and Viv on her own." For AO1, which AQA Level does this sit at, and why?
   * **Options:** A) Level 6 — a critical, exploratory, conceptualised response to task and whole text, B) Level 5 — a thoughtful, developed response with apt references integrated into interpretation, C) Level 3 — some explained response that stays at plot and biography rather than concept, D) Level 1 — a simple comment with no explanation
   * **Correct:** C
   * **AO:** AO1
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. AQA Level 6 AO1 rewards a "critical, exploratory, conceptualised response to task and whole text". Recounting Enid's arrival and jobs is plot-and-biography narration — AQA Level 3 "some explained response". To climb, the student must argue a CONCEPT (Pinnock using Enid to dramatise the cost of migration and the hunger for belonging), not narrate her life.
   * **Why A:** The conceptualised, critical argument the top band demands is absent — this recounts events, it does not argue an idea.
   * **Why B:** A "thoughtful, developed response" with "apt references integrated into interpretation" needs both development and quotation, and this has neither.
   * **Why D:** It is explained comment about the character's situation, not a bare simple comment, so it sits above the lowest band.

4. **Type: Ranking [Tests AO1]**

5. **Type: MCQ [Tests AO1]**

   * **Options:** A) It embeds more quotations to demonstrate the breadth of its evidence, B) It makes "judicious use of precise references to support interpretation" rather than general reference, C) It integrates the quotation with a clearly explained comment on its effect, D) It identifies the writer's method in the quotation using subject terminology
   * **Correct:** B
   * **AO:** AO1
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. AQA Level 6 AO1 is "judicious use of precise references to support interpretation(s)." "Judicious" means the reference is chosen to do argumentative work, not listed. A precise reference tied to a concept outweighs many general ones.

   * **Why C:** Explaining the effect of the quotation is AO2 work; the AO1 move is how the reference is SELECTED to support interpretation.
   * **Why D:** Naming the method and terminology is AO2 behaviour, not the AO1 distinction of judicious selection.
