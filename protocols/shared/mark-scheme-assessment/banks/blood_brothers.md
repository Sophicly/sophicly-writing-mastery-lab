# Mark Scheme Assessment Bank — Blood Brothers

Deterministic, code-scored MARK-SCHEME-LITERACY assessment bank (parsed by `SWML_Quiz_Bank::pick_session_msa`).
Examiner-level — harder + more precise than the Mark Scheme Quiz drill. Every question makes the
student JUDGE real writing about Blood Brothers against the EXACT mark-scheme descriptor wording, and forces
them to USE the mark scheme's own vocabulary rather than a simplified paraphrase. Keys + feedback live
server-side and are stripped before questions reach the client. The AI is never the scorekeeper.

AOs tagged per question for stratified random selection (the picker spreads the 10 across each board's own AO split).
Question types: MCQ · Select All · Fill · Ranking. Boards differ: AQA Modern Text = AO1+AO2+AO3 (plus AO4 SPaG,
4 marks); Edexcel GCSE Post-1914 = AO1+AO3 only, no AO2, with AO4 SPaG on a separate grid; Eduqas Post-1914 =
AO1+AO2 (plus AO4 SPaG, 5 marks), no AO3; OCR J352 = part (a) comparison AO1+AO2+AO3 (AO1 and AO3 equally
dominant), part (b) holistic AO1+AO2; Cambridge IGCSE 0475 = holistic best-fit — AO1 knowledge, AO2 understanding,
AO3 analysis of effects, AO4 personal response (NOT SPaG).

### **SECTION A: AQA (8702 — Modern Texts)**

1. **Type: MCQ [Tests AO1]**
   * **Question:** A student writes: "In Blood Brothers, Mickey and Eddie are twins who are separated at birth, grow up apart, become friends, and are both killed at the end." For AO1, which AQA Level does this response sit at, and why?
   * **Options:** A) Level 6 — a critical, exploratory, conceptualised response to task and whole text, B) Level 5 — a thoughtful, developed response with apt references integrated into interpretation, C) Level 3 — an explained response that narrates events rather than conceptualising them, D) Level 1 — a simple comment with no explanation
   * **Correct:** C
   * **AO:** AO1
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Tracing what the twins do across the play is plot-level narration — an explained response, not the "critical, exploratory, conceptualised response to task and whole text" AQA reserves for Level 6. To climb, treat the play as a vehicle for a concept (e.g. class deciding destiny), not a career to narrate.
   * **Why A:** The conceptualised, critical argument the top band demands is absent — this narrates a story, it does not argue an idea.
   * **Why B:** A "thoughtful, developed response" with "apt references integrated into interpretation" needs both development and quotation, and this has neither.
   * **Why D:** It is explained narration, not a bare "simple comment" — it sits above the lowest band.

   * **Correct:** B
   * **AO:** AO1
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. AQA Level 6 AO1 is "judicious use of precise references to support interpretation". "Judicious" means the reference is chosen to do argumentative work, not listed. A precise reference tied to a concept outweighs many general ones.
