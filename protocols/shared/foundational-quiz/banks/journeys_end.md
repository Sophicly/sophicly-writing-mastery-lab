### Quiz: Journey's End

1. **Type: MCQ [Tests Context]**
   * **Question:** *Journey's End* was written by R.C. Sherriff. What personal experience shaped its realistic portrayal of the trenches?
   * **Options:** A) He interviewed veterans but never served himself, B) His own service as an infantry officer on the Western Front during the First World War, C) He based it entirely on a novel he had read, D) He fought in the Second World War
   * **Correct:** B
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Sherriff had served as an officer in the First World War, and that first-hand experience gives the play its unflinching, authentic detail of dugout life.
   * **Why A:** Sherriff was not a mere observer; he had lived through trench warfare as a serving officer.
   * **Why C:** The play draws on his own memories of the front, not on someone else's novel.
   * **Why D:** The play concerns the First World War, which Sherriff lived through, not the later conflict.

   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. The play unfolds in a cramped dugout near St Quentin over a few days in March 1918, as the officers wait for the great German offensive they know is coming.
