### Quiz: IGCSE Literature Poetry

1. **Type: MCQ [Tests Identifying Form]**
   * **Question:** "Do not go gentle into that good night" by Dylan Thomas is written in which strict form?
   * **Options:** A) A villanelle, B) A sonnet, C) Free verse, D) A ballad
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. It is a villanelle — nineteen lines with two refrains that recur and combine, its circling repetition enacting a refusal to accept death.
   * **Why B:** It is nineteen lines with refrains, not a fourteen-line sonnet.
   * **Why C:** It follows strict villanelle rules, so it is not free verse.
   * **Why D:** A ballad tells a story in stanzas; this poem circles two refrains.

   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. A villanelle has nineteen lines and two refrains that recur and finally combine, creating a circling, obsessive effect.

11. **Type: MCQ [Tests Form Effects]**
   * **Question:** What is the effect of the villanelle's repeated refrains in "Do not go gentle into that good night"?

   * **Correct:** B
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. The forced return to "Do not go gentle" and "Rage, rage" makes the poem circle back, embodying a grief that cannot let go.
