1. **Type: MCQ [Tests Context]**
   * **Question:** Who wrote *A Taste of Honey*, famously producing it while still a teenager?

   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Shelagh Delaney wrote the play aged only around eighteen, drawing on the working-class Salford world she knew first-hand.

2. **Type: MCQ [Tests Context]**
   * **Question:** *A Taste of Honey* is regarded as a landmark of which theatrical movement?
   * **Options:** A) Kitchen-sink realism, B) Theatre of the Absurd, C) Restoration comedy, D) Ancient Greek tragedy
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. The play belongs to the "kitchen-sink" movement, which brought ordinary, working-class British life onto the stage with unflinching realism.
   * **Why B:** The Theatre of the Absurd dealt in surreal, illogical situations; Delaney's drama is rooted in gritty everyday reality.
   * **Why C:** Restoration comedy was the witty, aristocratic theatre of the seventeenth century, far from this play's world.
   * **Why D:** Though the story is unhappy, it is modern social realism, not the myth-based tragedy of ancient Greece.
