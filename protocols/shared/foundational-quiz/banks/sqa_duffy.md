### Quiz: SQA Duffy Poetry

1. **Type: MCQ [Tests Identifying Form]**
   * **Question:** "Anne Hathaway" by Carol Ann Duffy is written in which form?
   * **Options:** A) A Shakespearean sonnet, B) A ballad, C) Free verse, D) A villanelle
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. "Anne Hathaway" is a Shakespearean sonnet — fourteen lines closing in a rhyming couplet, the love form itself a fitting tribute to the playwright husband it addresses.
   * **Why B:** A ballad tells a story in song-like stanzas; this poem is a fourteen-line sonnet addressed to a husband.
   * **Why C:** The poem keeps the fourteen-line sonnet shape and a closing couplet, so it is not free verse.
   * **Why D:** A villanelle has nineteen lines and two repeating refrains; this poem has fourteen lines and no such refrains.

   * **Question:** In "Mrs Midas" the whole poem is spoken by Midas's wife, looking back on events in her own voice. This makes it which form?
   * **Options:** A) A dramatic monologue, B) A sonnet, C) A ballad, D) An ode
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. "Mrs Midas" is a dramatic monologue — the wife tells the story throughout, so we see the golden touch entirely through her bewilderment and grief.

   * **Why C:** A ballad narrates in tight song-like stanzas; this is one continuous personal account in the wife's voice.
   * **Why D:** An ode praises its subject; this poem mourns a ruined marriage rather than celebrating anything.

   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. A Shakespearean sonnet runs to fourteen lines and closes with a rhyming couplet — the shape Duffy borrows to honour Shakespeare himself.
