### Quiz: CCEA Relationships Poetry

1. **Type: MCQ [Tests Identifying Form]**
   * **Question:** "Sonnet 130" by William Shakespeare is written in which form?
   * **Options:** A) A Shakespearean sonnet, B) A ballad, C) Free verse, D) An ode
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Its fourteen lines catalogue the mistress against false compliment and close on the couplet "And yet, by heaven, I think my love as rare" — a Shakespearean sonnet.
   * **Why B:** It argues a case in fourteen lines rather than telling a story in song-like stanzas.
   * **Why C:** It keeps a strict rhyme and a fixed fourteen-line shape, so it is not free verse.
   * **Why D:** An ode praises its subject; this poem mocks flattery before prizing honest love.

2. **Type: MCQ [Tests Identifying Form]**
   * **Question:** "The Laboratory" by Robert Browning, in which one speaker addresses the poison-maker and lays bare her own mind, is best described as which form?
   * **Options:** A) A dramatic monologue, B) A sonnet, C) A villanelle, D) A choral ballad
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. A single speaker watches the apothecary at his "devil's-smithy" and betrays her gloating jealousy — the hallmark of a dramatic monologue.
   * **Why B:** It runs to many stanzas rather than the fourteen lines of a sonnet.
   * **Why C:** It has no two recurring refrains circling through nineteen lines, as a villanelle would.
   * **Why D:** It exposes one speaker's private mind rather than telling a communal, song-like tale.

   * **Why D:** It carries no two recurring refrains, as a villanelle demands.

10. **Type: MCQ [Tests Form Features]**
   * **Question:** The steady pairs of rhyming lines that build the argument of "To His Coy Mistress" and shape "On My First Son" are known as:
   * **Options:** A) Rhyming couplets, B) Refrains, C) A volta, D) Free verse
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Both poems run in rhyming couplets — paired end-rhymes that lend Marvell's argument its momentum and Jonson's elegy its measured control.
   * **Why B:** Refrains are whole repeated lines, as in a villanelle, not simple pairs of rhymes.
   * **Why C:** A volta is a single turn within a sonnet, not a rhyme pattern.
   * **Why D:** Free verse has no rhyme, yet both poems rhyme in ordered pairs.
