### Quiz: Time and Place Poetry

1. **Type: MCQ [Tests Identifying Form]**
   * **Question:** What form does Wordsworth use for "Composed upon Westminster Bridge"?
   * **Options:** A) A Petrarchan sonnet, B) A ballad, C) Free verse, D) A villanelle
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. It is a Petrarchan sonnet, its calm, ordered form capturing a tranquil early-morning view of the sleeping city.
   * **Why B:** It is a fourteen-line sonnet, not a narrative ballad.
   * **Why C:** It keeps sonnet form and rhyme, so it is not free verse.
   * **Why D:** A villanelle uses strict refrains, which this poem does not.

2. **Type: MCQ [Tests Identifying Form]**
   * **Question:** "To Autumn" by Keats is written as which form?
   * **Options:** A) A Horatian ode, B) A ballad, C) A dramatic monologue, D) Free verse
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. To Autumn is a Horatian ode — a formal poem of praise whose dense, rich language slows the reader into savouring the season.
   * **Why B:** A ballad is a narrative song form, unlike this ode of praise.
   * **Why C:** There is no single dramatic speaker exposing themselves to a listener.
   * **Why D:** Its formal, elevated structure is not free verse.
