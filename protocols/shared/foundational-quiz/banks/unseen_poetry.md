### Quiz: Unseen Poetry

1. **Type: MCQ [Tests Recognising Forms]**
   * **Question:** Which form is a fourteen-line poem built around a turn, or volta?
   * **Options:** A) The ballad, B) The sonnet, C) Free verse, D) The ode
   * **Correct:** B
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. A sonnet is fourteen lines with a volta — the turn where its argument or feeling shifts.
   * **Why A:** A ballad is a narrative song form, not defined by fourteen lines.
   * **Why C:** Free verse has no fixed length or turn.
   * **Why D:** An ode is a formal poem of praise, not fixed at fourteen lines.

2. **Type: MCQ [Tests Recognising Forms]**
   * **Question:** Which form is a poem of mourning for the dead, or for loss more broadly?
   * **Options:** A) An epic, B) An elegy, C) A limerick, D) A ballad
   * **Correct:** B
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. An elegy mourns the dead, or a lost way of life, often moving from grief towards consolation.
   * **Why A:** An epic is a long heroic narrative, not a poem of mourning.
   * **Why C:** A limerick is a short comic form.
   * **Why D:** A ballad tells a story in song form; mourning is the elegy's role.

3. **Type: MCQ [Tests Recognising Forms]**
   * **Question:** A poem spoken by a single character to a silent listener, who reveals themselves through what they say, is a...

   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. The dramatic monologue's single speaker and silent listener let the character expose themselves, often unintentionally.
   * **Why B:** A sonnet is defined by its fourteen-line structure, not a single dramatic speaker.
   * **Why C:** An ode is a formal poem of praise.
   * **Why D:** A villanelle is defined by its refrains, not by a speaker-and-listener setup.

11. **Type: MCQ [Tests Form Effects]**
   * **Question:** What effect does the repetition of a villanelle's refrains create?

   * **Correct:** B
