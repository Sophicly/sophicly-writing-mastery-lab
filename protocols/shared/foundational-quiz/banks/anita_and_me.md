   * **Question:** Meena's parents came to Britain from India. Which post-war reality does this reflect?

   * **Correct:** B
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Meena's family are part of the wave of Commonwealth migration to post-war Britain, which the novel draws on directly.

   * **Why D:** Meena's family settle in Britain rather than returning.
