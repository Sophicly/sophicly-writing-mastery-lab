### Quiz: Twelfth Night

1. **Type: MCQ [Tests Context]**
   * **Question:** To which dramatic genre does *Twelfth Night* belong?
   * **Options:** A) Tragedy, B) Romantic comedy, C) History play, D) Revenge tragedy
   * **Correct:** B
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. *Twelfth Night* is one of Shakespeare's mature romantic comedies, built on disguise, mistaken identity and a series of marriages that restore harmony by the close.
   * **Why A:** Though it carries an undercurrent of melancholy, the play ends in reconciliation and marriage, not death.
   * **Why C:** History plays dramatise the lives of English monarchs; this is a fictional comedy set in a faraway land.
   * **Why D:** There is no bloody vengeance plot; Malvolio's threatened "revenge" is played for comedy, not tragedy.

2. **Type: MCQ [Tests Context]**
   * **Question:** When did Shakespeare most likely write *Twelfth Night*?
   * **Options:** A) Around 1601–02, during the reign of Elizabeth I, B) In the 1370s, C) During the 1660s Restoration, D) In the 1850s
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. The play was written around 1601–02, near the end of Elizabeth I's reign, and belongs to the great run of comedies from Shakespeare's middle years.
   * **Why B:** Shakespeare was not yet born in the 1370s; he lived and wrote in the late sixteenth and early seventeenth centuries.
   * **Why C:** The Restoration of the 1660s came decades after Shakespeare's death in 1616.
   * **Why D:** The Victorian 1850s fall more than two centuries too late for a Shakespeare play.

7. **Type: MCQ [Tests Themes]**
   * **Question:** What does Orsino's opening line, "If music be the food of love, play on", suggest about his kind of love?
   * **Options:** A) That he loves Olivia selflessly and simply, B) That he is in love with the idea of love, feeding his own emotions and indulging his melancholy, C) That he cares nothing for Olivia, D) That he prefers music to any person
   * **Correct:** B
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Orsino wallows in his longing, treating love as an appetite to be fed and savoured — Shakespeare presents his passion as self-indulgent and changeable rather than deeply rooted.
   * **Why A:** His love is showy and self-absorbed; he is captivated by his own feelings more than by Olivia herself.
   * **Why C:** He is consumed by desire for Olivia, but it is an idealised, performative love.
   * **Why D:** Music is a means of feeding his mood of love, not a rival to it.
