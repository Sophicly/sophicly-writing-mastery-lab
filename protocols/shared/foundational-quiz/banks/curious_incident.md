   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Mark Haddon published this contemporary novel in 2003, and it quickly became a bestseller admired for the distinctive voice of its narrator.
   * **Why B:** Dickens was a Victorian writer; this is a modern novel of the early twenty-first century.

   * **Why D:** Steinbeck wrote earlier American fiction; this is a recent British novel.
