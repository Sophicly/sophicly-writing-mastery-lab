### Quiz: Power and Conflict Poetry

1. **Type: MCQ [Tests Identifying Form]**
   * **Question:** What form does Shelley use for "Ozymandias"?
   * **Options:** A) A ballad, B) A sonnet — used in a broken, ironic way, C) A villanelle, D) Free verse
   * **Correct:** B
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Ozymandias is a broken, irregular sonnet — Shelley bends the form of love and order to mock the impermanence of tyrannical power.
   * **Why A:** A ballad is a narrative song form; Ozymandias is a fourteen-line sonnet.
   * **Why C:** A villanelle has strict repeating refrains, which this poem does not use.
   * **Why D:** The poem keeps sonnet length and rhyme, so it is not free verse.

2. **Type: MCQ [Tests Identifying Form]**
   * **Question:** "My Last Duchess" by Browning is written as which form?
   * **Options:** A) A dramatic monologue, B) A sonnet, C) An elegy, D) A ballad
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. A single speaker, the Duke, addresses a silent listener and unwittingly exposes his own menace — the hallmark of a dramatic monologue.
   * **Why B:** It is a long speech, not a fourteen-line sonnet.
   * **Why C:** An elegy mourns the dead; the Duke's cold pride is not grief.
   * **Why D:** A ballad tells a story in song-like stanzas; this is one continuous speech.

   * **Correct:** B
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Its story-telling drive, repetition and pounding dactylic rhythm give it ballad-like momentum that memorialises the doomed charge.

   * **Why C:** It is a multi-stanza narrative, not a fourteen-line sonnet.
   * **Why D:** Its insistent metre and rhyme make it far from free verse.

7. **Type: MCQ [Tests Form Features]**
   * **Question:** How many lines does a sonnet have?
   * **Options:** A) Ten, B) Fourteen, C) Twenty, D) Eight
   * **Correct:** B
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. A sonnet is fourteen lines, usually with a turn (volta) between its opening problem and its resolution.
   * **Why A:** A sonnet has fourteen lines, not ten.
   * **Why C:** Twenty lines is longer than the fixed sonnet length.
   * **Why D:** Eight lines is only the octave — half of a Petrarchan sonnet.

8. **Type: MCQ [Tests Form Features]**
   * **Question:** What most defines free verse as a form?
   * **Options:** A) A strict fourteen-line shape, B) No fixed metre and no set rhyme scheme, C) A repeated refrain every stanza, D) Rhyming couplets throughout
   * **Correct:** B
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Free verse abandons regular metre and rhyme; that very freedom can itself carry meaning, such as authenticity or resistance.
   * **Why A:** A fixed fourteen-line shape describes the sonnet, not free verse.
   * **Why C:** A repeated refrain belongs to forms like the villanelle or ballad.
   * **Why D:** Consistent rhyming couplets are a regular scheme, the opposite of free verse.
