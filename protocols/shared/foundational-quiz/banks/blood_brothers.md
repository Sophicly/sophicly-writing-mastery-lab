### Quiz: Blood Brothers

1. **Type: MCQ [Tests Context]**
   * **Question:** The play is set largely in 1980s Liverpool. Which real conditions of that time shape it?
   * **Options:** A) A booming economy with full employment, B) Deindustrialisation and mass unemployment under Thatcher's government, C) A medieval feudal system, D) The Victorian railway boom
   * **Correct:** B
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Factory closures and high unemployment in Thatcher's Britain frame Mickey's decline and the play's anger about class and opportunity.
   * **Why A:** The backdrop is unemployment and decline, not a boom.
   * **Why C:** It is set in modern industrial Liverpool, not a feudal world.
   * **Why D:** The context is late-twentieth-century decline, not Victorian expansion.

2. **Type: MCQ [Tests Context]**

   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. The play dramatises the "Two Nations" tradition — the gulf between the wealthy Lyons and the struggling Johnstones.
   * **Why B:** The contrast is social, not scientific.
   * **Why C:** It concerns class division, not the authority of kings.
   * **Why D:** The Chain of Being belongs to a much earlier period and worldview.

   * **WhyWrong:** Russell did write with this purpose — the twins' contrasting fates argue that class, not merit, shapes a person's opportunities.
