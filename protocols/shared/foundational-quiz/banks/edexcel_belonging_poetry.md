   * **Question:** "Island Man" by Grace Nichols is written in which form?
   * **Options:** A) Free verse, B) A Petrarchan sonnet, C) A ballad, D) A villanelle
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Nichols uses free verse, its drifting, unpunctuated flow mirroring the man's half-waking movement between his island home and London.

7. **Type: MCQ [Tests Form Features]**
   * **Question:** What most defines free verse as a form?
   * **Options:** A) No fixed metre and no set rhyme scheme, B) Exactly fourteen lines, C) A repeated refrain every stanza, D) Rhyming couplets throughout
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Free verse abandons regular metre and rhyme; that freedom can itself carry meaning, such as authenticity or a modern, personal voice.
   * **Why B:** A fixed fourteen-line shape describes the sonnet.
   * **Why C:** A refrain every stanza points to forms like the villanelle or ballad.
   * **Why D:** Consistent rhyming couplets are a regular scheme, the opposite of free verse.
