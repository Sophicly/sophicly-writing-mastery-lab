1. **Type: MCQ [Tests Identifying Form]**
   * **Question:** What form does Shelley use for "Ozymandias"?
   * **Options:** A) A sonnet, used in a broken, ironic way, B) A ballad, C) Free verse, D) A villanelle
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Ozymandias is a broken sonnet — Shelley bends the ordered form to mock the impermanence of tyrannical power.
   * **Why B:** A ballad is a narrative song form, unlike this fourteen-line sonnet.

   * **Why D:** A villanelle uses strict refrains, which this poem does not.
