### Quiz: CCEA Conflict Poetry

1. **Type: MCQ [Tests Identifying Form]**
   * **Question:** "Anthem for Doomed Youth" by Wilfred Owen is written in which form?
   * **Options:** A) A sonnet, B) A ballad, C) Free verse, D) A villanelle
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Owen writes "Anthem for Doomed Youth" as a sonnet — fourteen lines whose octave answers its opening question with the sounds of war before the sestet turns home to quiet mourning.
   * **Why B:** A ballad tells a story in song-like stanzas, but this poem is a single fourteen-line meditation rather than a narrative.
   * **Why C:** The poem keeps a regular fourteen-line shape with rhyme and a turn, so it is not free verse.
   * **Why D:** A villanelle runs to nineteen lines built on two refrains, unlike this compact fourteen-line poem.

2. **Type: MCQ [Tests Identifying Form]**
   * **Question:** "What lips my lips have kissed, and where, and why" by Edna St Vincent Millay is which form?
   * **Options:** A) A sonnet, B) A dramatic monologue, C) A ballad, D) An ode
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Millay's poem is a sonnet — fourteen lines whose octave recalls forgotten lovers before the turn at "Thus in winter stands the lonely tree" likens the speaker to a bare, silent tree.
   * **Why B:** A dramatic monologue addresses a silent listener, whereas this is an inward, fourteen-line reflection.
   * **Why C:** A ballad narrates a story in stanzas, but this poem meditates rather than telling events.
   * **Why D:** An ode sustains praise of its subject, while this poem mourns what has been lost.

7. **Type: MCQ [Tests Form Features]**
   * **Question:** Which of these best describes a sonnet?
   * **Options:** A) A fourteen-line poem, usually turning at a volta, B) A nineteen-line poem built on two refrains, C) A single three-line unrhymed stanza, D) A narrative poem defined by a refrain
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. A sonnet runs to fourteen lines and usually pivots at a volta, where its argument or feeling turns — as in Owen's "Anthem for Doomed Youth".
   * **Why B:** A nineteen-line poem built on two refrains describes the villanelle, not the sonnet.
   * **Why C:** A three-line unrhymed stanza is a tercet, a building block rather than a whole form.
   * **Why D:** A poem defined by a recurring refrain points to a ballad, not the fourteen-line sonnet.

10. **Type: MCQ [Tests Form Features]**

   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. A quatrain is a four-line stanza — the unit Hardy uses five times over in "The Man He Killed".
