### Quiz: Worlds and Lives Poetry

1. **Type: MCQ [Tests Identifying Form]**
   * **Question:** "England in 1819" by Shelley is written as which form?
   * **Options:** A) A sonnet, used in an inverted, political way, B) A ballad, C) Free verse, D) A villanelle
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Shelley uses an inverted sonnet — piling up the nation's ills before turning, late, towards hope — to give political critique the weight of the form.
   * **Why B:** A ballad is a narrative song form, unlike this fourteen-line political poem.
   * **Why C:** It keeps sonnet length and rhyme, so it is not free verse.
   * **Why D:** A villanelle uses strict repeating refrains, which this poem does not.

2. **Type: MCQ [Tests Identifying Form]**
   * **Question:** "Lines Written in Early Spring" by Wordsworth is best described as a...
   * **Options:** A) Pastoral lyric, B) Dramatic monologue, C) Epic, D) Villanelle
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. It is a pastoral lyric — a meditative poem of nature that links the beauty of the natural world to human feeling.
   * **Why B:** There is no single dramatic speaker exposing themselves to a listener.
   * **Why C:** It is a short reflective poem, not a long heroic epic.
