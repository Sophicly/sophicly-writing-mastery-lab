### Quiz: OCR Youth and Age Poetry

1. **Type: MCQ [Tests Identifying Form]**
   * **Question:** Robert Frost's 'Out, Out–' tells of the buzz-saw and the farm boy in unbroken, unrhymed lines of roughly ten syllables. Which form is this?
   * **Options:** A) Blank verse, B) A ballad, C) A villanelle, D) A pantoum
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. The lines run without rhyme and without stanza breaks, at roughly ten syllables each — blank verse, whose bare plainness suits the sudden, matter-of-fact horror of the accident.
   * **Why B:** A ballad tells its story in rhymed, song-like stanzas, whereas this poem runs unbroken and unrhymed.
   * **Why C:** A villanelle turns on nineteen lines and two repeating refrains, none of which appear here.
   * **Why D:** A pantoum repeats whole lines in an interlocking pattern; Frost's narrative never circles back on its own words.

2. **Type: MCQ [Tests Identifying Form]**
   * **Question:** In Raymond Antrobus's 'Happy Birthday Moon', the second and fourth lines of each stanza return as the first and third of the next. Which form is this?
   * **Options:** A) A pantoum, B) A sonnet, C) Free verse, D) A dramatic monologue
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Whole lines repeat in an interlocking chain, and the opening line comes back at the close — this weaving of returning lines is the pantoum.
   * **Why B:** A sonnet is a single fourteen-line poem with a turn, not a poem built from returning, repeated lines.
   * **Why C:** Free verse follows no fixed pattern, yet this poem's lines recur in a strict, planned order.
   * **Why D:** A dramatic monologue is defined by a single speaker addressing a silent listener, not by repeated lines.

10. **Type: MCQ [Tests Form Features]**
   * **Question:** In 'Baby Song', Gunn writes in rhyming couplets. What is a rhyming couplet?
   * **Options:** A) A pair of consecutive lines that rhyme with each other, B) A stanza of four lines rhyming alternately, C) A line of ten unrhymed syllables, D) A refrain repeated at the end of each stanza
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. A rhyming couplet is simply two lines side by side that rhyme, the tightest of the rhymed units.
   * **Why B:** A four-line stanza rhyming alternately is a quatrain, not a couplet.
   * **Why C:** A line of ten unrhymed syllables points to blank verse, which by definition does not rhyme.
   * **Why D:** A refrain repeated at each stanza's end belongs to forms such as the villanelle, not the couplet.
