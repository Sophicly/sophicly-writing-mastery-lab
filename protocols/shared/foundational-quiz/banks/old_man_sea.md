### Quiz: The Old Man and the Sea

1. **Type: MCQ [Tests Context]**
   * **Question:** In which year was *The Old Man and the Sea* first published?
   * **Options:** A) 1952, B) 1899, C) 1925, D) 1961
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Hemingway published the novella in 1952; it was the last major work of fiction to appear in his lifetime and revived his literary reputation.
   * **Why B:** 1899 is the year of Hemingway's birth, not the book's publication.
   * **Why C:** The mid-1920s belong to Hemingway's early career; this is a late work.
   * **Why D:** 1961 is the year of Hemingway's death, nearly a decade after the novella appeared.

2. **Type: MCQ [Tests Context]**
   * **Question:** Where is *The Old Man and the Sea* set?
   * **Options:** A) Off the coast of Cuba in the Gulf Stream, B) Off the coast of Florida in the Atlantic, C) In the Mediterranean Sea, D) Off the northern coast of Spain
   * **Correct:** A
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Santiago fishes out of a small village near Havana, rowing far into the Gulf Stream off the Cuban coast, where the great marlin takes his line.
   * **Why B:** The village and the fishermen are Cuban, though the Gulf Stream runs on towards Florida.
   * **Why C:** The waters are the Caribbean off Cuba, not the Mediterranean.
   * **Why D:** Hemingway loved Spain, but this story unfolds in Cuban waters.

4. **Type: MCQ [Tests Context]**
   * **Question:** Hemingway is celebrated for a distinctive prose style. How is it best described?
   * **Options:** A) Ornate and elaborate, built from long, decorative sentences, B) Spare and understated, leaving much of the meaning beneath the surface — his "iceberg" style, C) Written almost entirely in local dialect, D) Crowded with long philosophical digressions
   * **Correct:** B
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Hemingway's so-called iceberg theory keeps the prose plain and pared down, so that the deepest feeling lies unstated beneath simple words and short sentences.
   * **Why A:** The style is deliberately plain, the opposite of ornate decoration.
   * **Why C:** Dialect is not the defining feature; disciplined simplicity is.
   * **Why D:** Hemingway strips such digressions away, trusting understatement to carry the weight.

7. **Type: MCQ [Tests Themes]**
   * **Question:** What does Santiago's struggle with the marlin and the sharks reveal about Hemingway's view of the human condition?
   * **Options:** A) That effort is ultimately pointless, B) That a person may lose the material prize yet keep dignity and courage through endurance, C) That only outright victory gives life meaning, D) That nature always rewards hard work
   * **Correct:** B
   * **Feedback:** ✓ Correct. Though the sharks strip the marlin to bone, Santiago endures with dignity; his conviction that "a man can be destroyed but not defeated" captures Hemingway's belief in grace under pressure.
   * **Why A:** The novella honours the struggle itself as meaningful, whatever the outcome.
   * **Why C:** Santiago loses the fish yet is not defeated — spirit outlasts the result.
   * **Why D:** Nature is indifferent here; the marlin is lost despite all his labour.
