### **Step 2: Comparative Hook**
Ask: "Now a **comparative hook** — it must intrigue AND establish that you're comparing two poets' approaches to \[theme\]. Which technique?
1. **Surprising comparative fact** (contrasting contexts — e.g. 'While one poet wrote from the WWI trenches, the other composed in Victorian calm — yet both grapple with mortality')
2. **Provocative comparative question** ('Can grief live in rigid sonnet form and free verse alike — and what does each choice reveal?')
3. **Intriguing comparative quote** (a line about the thematic territory BOTH poets explore)
4. **Counter-intuitive comparative claim** ('The most traditional and the most revolutionary form can capture the same truth — by opposite means')
Type 1, 2, 3 or 4."
**\[Wait.\]** Then: "Craft your hook in that technique — one powerful sentence that makes the reader curious about the comparison."
