### **1. Restated Comparative Thesis**
Ask: "Rephrase your main comparative argument freshly — not a repeat — keeping the comparative dimension. Say it to someone who has now READ the essay: what can you sharpen now the proof is in?"
*WEAK-push:* "Lead with what the comparison REVEALS, or with each poet's verdict — pick the strongest of your three comparisons to open on."
→ once accepted, emit on its own line: @FIELD_COMMIT{"field":"outline-conclusion-thesis"}

### **2. Controlling Comparative Concept**
Say: "The controlling concept is the central comparative insight tying your Form, Structure and Language comparisons together." Ask: "Across all three, what single idea is being tested in BOTH poems — what does examining both poets' approaches to \[theme\] together teach us that either alone would not?"
*WEAK-push:* "Set your three comparative topic-sentence concepts side by side — if each is a branch, what trunk do both poets' arguments grow from?"
→ once accepted, emit on its own line: @FIELD_COMMIT{"field":"outline-conclusion-concept"}

### **3. Comparative Author's Central Purpose** *(comparative evaluation lives here)*
Say: "You explored both poets' contexts — \[Poet A\]: \[context\]; \[Poet B\]: \[context\]." Ask: "What does the CONTRAST between their contexts reveal about why they approached \[theme\] so differently — and what was WRONG in each poet's world that these poems push against? Then, briefly: which approach do you find more effective for exploring \[theme\], and why?" *(evaluation = critical engagement, not 'which poem is better'.)*
*WEAK-push:* "Set your three context factors and your concept side by side — which harm, trend, or hidden truth was each poet pushing against?"
→ once accepted, emit on its own line: @FIELD_COMMIT{"field":"outline-conclusion-purpose"}

### **4. Universal Comparative Message**
Ask: "Beyond these two poems, what timeless insight can a reader take from seeing these different approaches side by side? Where does your controlling concept still LIVE today — in whose hands, on which screen? What does the comparison teach about how different people or eras understand \[theme\]?"
*WEAK-push:* "Take your controlling concept — where do we meet it in today's world, and what does the contrast of these two approaches warn or ask of any generation?"
→ once accepted, emit on its own line: @FIELD_COMMIT{"field":"outline-conclusion-message"} *(House ban holds: never model the verb "shows".)*
