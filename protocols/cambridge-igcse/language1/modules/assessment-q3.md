# ASSESSMENT — PAPER 1, QUESTION 3 [25]
## Extended response in role, from Text C · 15 reading + 10 writing · 250–350 words

The task names a **role**, an **audience**, a **form** and **three bullets**. Forms observed across
40 papers: speech 8 · letter 8 · journal 7 · interview 6 · article 5 · report 2.

## THE TWO TABLES, AND WHAT ACTUALLY MOVES THEM

**Reading [15] — this is where the marks are, and where VOICE is scored.**
Every level ends on voice: *"A consistent and convincing voice"* (L5) → *"An appropriate voice"*
(L4) → *"The voice is plain"* (L3) → *"The voice might be inappropriate"* (L2).
⭐ **Tell the student explicitly that voice is a READING mark.** Almost every student assumes it is
a writing mark and spends their effort on the wrong ten.

**Bullet coverage is the structural criterion, and it is marked:**
L5 *"All three bullets are well covered"* → L4 *"All three bullets are covered"* →
L3 *"There is uneven focus on the bullets"* → L2 *"One of the bullets may not be addressed."*
**Always report coverage bullet by bullet** — it is objective and the student can act on it.

**The single biggest lever is development.** Level 3 is *"Opportunities for development are rarely
taken."* Level 5 is *"Developed ideas are sustained and well related to the text."* Level 1 is *"a
reproduction of sections of the original"* and *"little realisation of the need to modify material
from the text."*

## MARK AGAINST TOGA — the four moves, once per bullet

Sophicly teaches this question as **Take it · Own it · Grow it · Aim it**, run once per bullet
(`TOGA-one-technique-for-every-form.md`). Mark against it, because each move maps to a descriptor:

| move | what to check | descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| **Take** | is there a real, specific detail from Text C in this bullet? | *"supporting detail throughout, which is well integrated"* |
| **Own** | is it recast, or lifted? | L1 *"a reproduction of sections of the original"* |
| **Grow** | is anything ADDED that the role would know and the text only implies? | L3 *"Opportunities for development are rarely taken"* |
| **Aim** | does it serve this reader and purpose, in a held voice? | *"a strong sense of purpose"*, *"a consistent and convincing voice"* |

**In the feedback, mark T · O · G · A per bullet and name the missing one.** A response showing
T and O but no G is sitting at Level 3, and this tells the student exactly why in one letter.

## FORM — worth about five lines, and no more

⚠️ **There is NO form-specific descriptor anywhere in either table.** Table B asks for *"effective
register for audience and purpose"* and never mentions salutations, headlines, datelines or
sign-offs. So: check the opening, the closing and that the register holds between them — and do
**not** invent form rules the mark scheme does not contain, or spend feedback on layout.

## WHAT TO GIVE BACK, EVERY TIME

1. **Reading /15 and Writing /10, each with its band descriptor quoted**, then the total /25.
2. **Bullet-by-bullet coverage**, with T·O·G·A marked and the missing move named.
3. **A voice verdict in one sentence** — is this person recognisably speaking, and where does the
   voice slip?
4. **The student's weakest bullet rewritten to gold standard**, in their role and their form, using
   their own material — so the gain is visible on their own work.
5. **One alternative optimal approach** to the same bullet, showing a different Grow they could
   have reached for from Text C.
6. **Every penalty carries a fix-example.** Never say *develop your ideas more* without writing the
   developed sentence.
