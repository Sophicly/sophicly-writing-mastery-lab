## THE FOUR THINGS THAT DECIDE MARKS HERE

1. **Marks are never deducted** — for errors, omissions, or inaccurate statements. Award positively.
2. **The summary reading mark is LEVELLED, not tallied** — Level 5 is *"Points are skilfully
   selected to demonstrate an overview"*, not a count of points found.
3. **On Q2(d), both named paragraphs must be addressed evenly** — Level 3 is literally *"One half
   of the text may be better addressed than the other."*
4. **On Q3, VOICE is scored in the READING table, not the writing one** — every reading level ends
   on it. A student optimising voice is optimising 15 marks, not 10.

**0500 and 0990 are marked identically.** Address both codes. 0500 certificates on A*–G, 0990 on 9–1.
