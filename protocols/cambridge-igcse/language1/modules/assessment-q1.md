# ASSESSMENT — PAPER 1, QUESTION 1 [30]
## 1(a)–(e) comprehension on Text A [15] · 1(f) summary of Text B [15]

## PART ONE — 1(a)–(e), the short answers [15]

Mark each part **to its own printed tariff** (`1,2,2,2,2,3,3` across the parts, 15 total). These are
right-or-wrong retrieval and own-words explanation, so they take **a mark and a one-line reason** —
**no gold-standard rewrite** (that is reserved for the extended tasks).

For each part, give: **the mark · what was creditable · what was missing.** Where a mark was lost,
state the exact words that would have earned it — never *be more precise* with no example.

**The own-words parts** *("Using your own words, explain what the text means by…")* fail in one
predictable way: the student repeats the quoted phrase inside the answer. Name that when it happens
and show the recast.

## PART TWO — 1(f), the summary [15] = 10 reading + 5 writing

⭐ **THE READING MARK IS LEVELLED, NOT TALLIED.** Do not count points and convert. Read the whole
response and place it in a band. The Level 5 discriminator is *"Points are skilfully selected to
demonstrate an overview"* — a response listing many correct details with no overview is **Level 3**
(*"may include excess material"*), not Level 5.

⚠️ **The Writing table has only THREE levels, maximum 5 marks.** There is no "Level 5" for summary
writing. State this if the student asks why their summary writing mark looks low against the others.

**The 120-word limit is a stated MAXIMUM** and the examiner report confirms overruns cost marks —
unlike the Q2(d) and Q3 word guidance, which are not caps. Count the words, report the count, and if
it is over, show which sentence to cut and why that one.

### What to give back, every time

1. **Reading mark /10 with the band descriptor quoted**, and the one sentence that decides it.
2. **Writing mark /5 with the band descriptor quoted.**
3. **The overview test** — in one sentence, what the summary should have *concluded* about Text B.
   Most Level 3 summaries have the material and no overview; this is the single biggest lever.
4. **A gold-standard summary** of their own points, inside 120 words, in continuous prose.
5. **Their own words check** — flag any lifted phrase and show the recast beside it.
