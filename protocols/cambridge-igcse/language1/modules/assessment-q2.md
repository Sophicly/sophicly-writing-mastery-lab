# ASSESSMENT — PAPER 1, QUESTION 2 [25]
## 2(a)–(c) on Text C [10] · 2(d) Writer's Effect [15]

## PART ONE — 2(a)–(c) [10]

`2(a)(i–iv)` identify a word or phrase suggesting a given idea [1 each]; `2(b)(i–iii)` explain
underlined words in own words [1 each]; `2(c)` explain how ONE given example suggests a feeling [3].

Mark to tariff, one line of reason each. On 2(b), a dictionary definition that ignores the context
does not earn the mark — show the contextual meaning instead.

## PART TWO — 2(d), the Writer's Effect [15]

⭐ **MARK HOLISTICALLY. The mark scheme says so in its own words:** *"Mark holistically for the
overall quality of the response, not for the number of words chosen… It is the quality of the
analysis that attracts marks."*

⛔ **DO NOT DEDUCT FOR WRONG COMMENTS.** *"Do not deduct marks for inaccurate statements; simply
ignore them."* Ignore, then teach.

### The band separators, in the mark scheme's own words

- **Level 5 (13–15):** *"high-quality comments that add meaning and associations to words/phrases
  **in both parts of the text**"* · *"Tackles imagery with some precision and imagination."*
- **Level 4 (10–12):** effects identified **in both parts of the text**.
- **Level 3 (7–9):** *"any attempt to suggest and explain effects is basic or very general"* ·
  ⭐ *"**One half of the text may be better addressed than the other.**"*

⭐ **THE BOTH-PARAGRAPHS RULE IS THE COMMONEST CEILING.** The question names **two** paragraphs.
A response that analyses the first richly and the second thinly is **Level 3 by descriptor**,
however good the first half is. **Always report the balance** — how many choices came from each
named paragraph — because the student cannot see it themselves.

### What to give back, every time

1. **The mark /15 with the band descriptor quoted**, and the balance count (paragraph 1 vs 2).
2. **Per choice:** the word or phrase, whether it was *powerful or unusual* enough to be worth
   choosing, and whether the comment reached **effect** or stopped at **meaning**. Meaning-only is
   the Level 2–3 signature.
3. **Imagery** — was at least one image tackled, as the question requires? Level 5 needs it.
4. **A gold-standard analysis of ONE of their own choices**, showing meaning → association →
   effect on the reader, so the improvement is visible on their own material.
5. **One alternative optimal choice** they missed from the weaker paragraph, with the analysis
   written out.

⚠️ Non-vocabulary comments (grammar, syntax, punctuation) **are creditable, but only as additions**
to vocabulary comment — never as a substitute. And on stylistic effect the rule is verbatim: *"Only credit comments on stylistic effect where
these are explicitly linked to choices."*
