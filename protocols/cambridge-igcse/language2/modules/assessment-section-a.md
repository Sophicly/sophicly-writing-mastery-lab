# ASSESSMENT — PAPER 2, SECTION A, QUESTION 1 [40]
## Directed writing from TWO texts · 15 reading + 25 writing · 250–350 words

Form is always **letter, speech or article**. Bullet 1 is always *"evaluate the ideas, attitudes and
opinions given in both texts."* Bullet 2 is the student's own view, advice, or the effects.

## THE READING TABLE [15] — the ladder is EVALUATION

L6 *"Successfully evaluates ideas and opinions, both explicit and implicit"* · *"**Assimilates**
ideas from the text to give a developed, sophisticated response"* → L5 same evaluation, *"a thorough
response, supported by a detailed selection"* → L4 *"Begins to evaluate **mainly explicit** ideas"*
→ L3 *"**Selects and comments on** explicit ideas"* → L2 *"**Identifies** explicit ideas."*

⭐ **The verb ladder IS the mark ladder**: identifies → comments → begins to evaluate → evaluates
explicit *and implicit* → assimilates. **Report which verb the response actually performs.** If
every reference is *"Text A says…"*, the response is commenting, and the table prices that at 5–6.

⭐ **"Implicit" is the gate to the top two levels** and students almost never cross it unprompted.
Name at least one implicit idea in the texts that the response missed, and write the sentence that
would have evaluated it.

## THE WRITING TABLE [25] — the trap is STRUCTURE

⛔ **Level 3 is *"Relies on the sequence of the original text."*** A response that walks through
Text A then Text B is capped at **10–13 of 25** however fluent the prose. This is the most valuable
single thing to tell a Cambridge student, and it is invisible to them.

**Always report the ORDER the response used**: text-led (A then B) or argument-led (a claim per
paragraph, both texts inside each). Argument-led is what *"carefully structured for benefit of the
reader"* means here.

## MARK AGAINST TOGA, WITH ONE VERB SWAPPED

Sophicly teaches this as **Take it · Own it · WEIGH it · Aim it**, once per bullet — the same engine
as Paper 1 Q3 with *Grow* replaced by *Weigh*, because this table ladders on evaluation rather than
development (`protocols/cambridge-igcse/method/TOGA-one-technique-for-every-form.md`). **Weigh** =
judge the claim and say which text is more convincing and why.

## WHAT TO GIVE BACK, EVERY TIME

1. **Reading /15 and Writing /25, each with its band descriptor quoted**, then the total /40.
2. **The verb verdict** — identifies / comments / begins to evaluate / evaluates / assimilates —
   with the sentence that proves it.
3. **The order verdict** — text-led or argument-led, and if text-led, the argument-led plan it
   should have used, written out as three claim sentences.
4. **One implicit idea missed**, with the evaluating sentence written for them.
5. **Both bullets checked.** Bullet 2 must be built from ideas the evaluation left standing; advice
   from nowhere scores like a text-free essay.
6. **A gold-standard rewrite of ONE paragraph** using their own material, plus **one alternative
   optimal approach**.
7. **Every penalty carries a fix-example.**

⛔ **Do not import a six-part non-fiction essay scaffold here.** It is built for 700 words; this task
allows 250–350, and it contains no slot for the evaluation that carries 15 of the 40 marks.
