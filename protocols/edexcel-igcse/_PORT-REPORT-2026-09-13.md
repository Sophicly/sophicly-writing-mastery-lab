### The one change of substance I made, and why

**Paper 2 Q1's element split was re-cut so the AO decomposition is EXACT.** The board marks Section A
on two grids — AO1 out of 12 and AO2 out of 18 — and the student's official mark is the pair. The
previous split (Intro 3 + 3 × 7 + Conclusion 6, with mixed `AO1/AO2` rows) summed to 30 but decomposed
to **AO1 14.0 / AO2 12.5 / 3.5 shared**, which cannot produce the board's two marks; any AO-split
figure printed from it would have been an estimate dressed as arithmetic (A6 violation). Every element
now carries exactly ONE AO and each set sums to its grid maximum:

- Introduction **3.0** = AO1 2.0 + AO2 1.0
- each body paragraph **7.0** = AO1 2.5 + AO2 4.5 (× 3 = AO1 7.5 + AO2 13.5)
- Conclusion **6.0** = AO1 2.5 + AO2 3.5
- **AO1 12.0 · AO2 18.0 · question 30.0.**

The paragraph TOTALS (3 / 7 / 7 / 7 / 6) are unchanged, so the document scaffold's section worths still
match. What changed is the per-element worths and their AO labels inside those totals. Consequence for
the engine lane: **the outline element set for P2 Q1 body paragraphs is now 8 rows, not 10** — see
§4 (JS-ROWS SPEC) row 4, and note that outline shape is baked into saved documents and needs the
