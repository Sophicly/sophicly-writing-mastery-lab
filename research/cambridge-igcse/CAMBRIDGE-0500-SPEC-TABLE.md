## 1. QUALIFICATION SHAPE

- **No tiering.** No Core/Extended. Candidates are eligible for **grades A*–G**.
- **All candidates take Paper 1, plus EITHER Paper 2 OR Component 3.**
- **Component 4** (Speaking and Listening) is **optional and separately endorsed** — its marks
  **do not contribute to the overall grade**. Certificated separately, grades 1 (high) to 5 (low).

| component | duration | raw marks | weight | assessed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Paper 1 — Reading** (compulsory) | 2 hours | **80** | 50% | externally |
| **Paper 2 — Directed Writing and Composition** | 2 hours | **80** | 50% | externally |
| *or* **Component 3 — Coursework Portfolio** | — | 80 | 50% | internally assessed, externally moderated |
| **Component 4 — Speaking and Listening** (optional) | ~10–12 min | 40 | separately endorsed | internally assessed, externally moderated |

❓ **NEEDS NEIL:** is the student entered for **Paper 2** or **Component 3**? Coursework is a
completely different deliverable (three 500–800-word assignments, first draft submitted, texts
attached) and we should not build it unless it is the route.

## 2. ASSESSMENT OBJECTIVES — ⚠️ NOT AQA's SCHEME. Do not carry AQA AO numbers across.

Cambridge has **three** AOs, and they name **skills**, not the AQA reading/writing sub-objectives:

- **AO1 Reading** — sub-strands **R1–R5**
- **AO2 Writing** — sub-strands **W1–W5**
- **AO3 Speaking and Listening** — sub-strands **SL1–SL5**

Verbatim sub-strands (syllabus p.9):

| code | wording |
|---|---|
| R1 | demonstrate understanding of explicit meanings |
| R2 | demonstrate understanding of implicit meanings and attitudes |
| R3 | analyse, evaluate and develop facts, ideas and opinions, using appropriate support from the text |
| R4 | demonstrate understanding of how writers achieve effects and influence readers |
| R5 | select and use information for specific purposes |
| W1 | articulate experience and express what is thought, felt and imagined |
| W2 | organise and structure ideas and opinions for deliberate effect |
| W3 | use a range of vocabulary and sentence structures appropriate to context |
| W4 | use register appropriate to context |
| W5 | make accurate use of spelling, punctuation and grammar |

**Weightings**

| AO | % of qualification | Paper 1 | Paper 2 / Component 3 | Component 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AO1 Reading | 50 | **80** | 20 | 0 |
| AO2 Writing | 50 | **20** | **80** | 0 |
| AO3 Speaking & Listening | separately endorsed | 0 | 0 | 100 |

⭐ **Note the shape this forces on us:** Paper 1 is 80% reading but still carries **20% writing
marks**, and Paper 2 is 80% writing but still carries **20% reading marks**. Neither paper is
"pure". Any protocol that marks Paper 2 as writing-only loses a fifth of the paper.

## 3. PAPER 1 — READING (2 h, 80 marks)

**Three texts**, which may be on a similar topic. Candidates answer **three compulsory questions**.
- Text A and Text B: **700–750 words** each. Text C: **500–650 words**.
- Candidates should spend **approximately 15 minutes reading**.
- Answers written in the space provided on the question paper. **Dictionaries may not be used.**

| Q | task | responds to | marks | AO split |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Comprehension and summary task** | | **30** | |
| 1 · part a | Comprehension: series of sub-questions, short answers, explicit **and** implicit meanings | **Text A** | 15 | R1, R2, R5 |
| 1 · part b | **Summary**, selective, **in own words**, continuous writing of **no more than 120 words** | **Text B** | 15 | **10 reading** (R1, R2, R5) + **5 writing** (W2, W3) |
| **2** | **Short-answer questions and language task** | | **25** | |
| 2 · part a | Short-answer sub-questions, answers of differing lengths | **Text C** | 10 | R1, R2, R4 |
| 2 · part b | **Language task** — about **200–300 words** | **Text C** | 15 | R1, R2, R4 |
| **3** | **Extended response to reading** — about **250–350 words**, written in one of: **letter, report, journal, speech, interview, article** | **Text C** | **25** | **15 reading** (R1, R2, R3) + **10 writing** (W1, W2, W3, W4) |

### 3b. How Paper 1 is actually marked — the granular spine (from the 2024 mark scheme)

This is what our MSA bank and marking protocol must reproduce. **Two different marking modes in one
paper** — itemised point-marking for the short answers, level-descriptor tables for the long ones.

| item | mode | marks | mark scheme reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1(a) | point-marked | 1 | R5 |
| 1(b)(i) / 1(b)(ii) | point-marked, "using your own words, explain what the text means by…" | 2 + 2 | R1 |
| 1(c) | point-marked | 2 | R2 |
| 1(d)(i) / 1(d)(ii) | point-marked | 2 + 3 | R1, R5 |
| 1(e) | point-marked | 3 | R1, R2 |
| **1(f) summary** | **level descriptors** | **15** | **Table A Reading /10** + **Table B Writing /5** |
| 2(a)(i)–(iv), 2(b)(i)–(iii) | point-marked, 1 each | 7 | R1 / R2 |
| 2(c) | point-marked | 3 | R2, R4 |
| **2(d) language task** | **level descriptors** | **15** | **Table A Reading: "Analysing how writers achieve effects" /15** |
| **3 extended response** | **level descriptors** | **25** | **Table A Reading /15** + **Table B Writing: "Structure and order, style of language" /10** |
| | | **80** | |

⭐ **Design consequence for our engine:** the point-marked sub-questions are right-or-wrong retrieval
— by the `CLAUDE.md` universal per-paragraph rule they are the **Q1-excluded** shape (score +
per-item feedback, **no gold-standard rewrite**). Only 1(f), 2(d) and 3 get the full four-part
treatment (mark · feedback · rewrite to gold · alternative model). Do not apply TTECEA to the
point-marked items.

## 4. PAPER 2 — DIRECTED WRITING AND COMPOSITION (2 h, 80 marks)

Two questions, **one from each section**. Texts printed on the insert. **Dictionaries may not be used.**

### Section A — Directed Writing (40 marks) — ⚠️ **THIS SECTION HAS SOURCE TEXTS**
- One **compulsory** question on **one or two texts totalling 650–750 words**.
- Candidates **use, develop and evaluate the information in the text(s)** to create a
  **discursive / argumentative / persuasive speech, letter or article**.
- Candidates write **about 250–350 words**.
- **Marking:** **Table A — Writing, out of 25** (W1–W5) · **Table B — Reading, out of 15** (R1, R2, R3, R5).

### Section B — Composition (40 marks)
- One question from a choice of **four titles: two descriptive and two narrative**.
- Candidates write **about 350–450 words**.
- **Marking:** **Table A — Content and structure, out of 16** · **Table B — Style and accuracy, out of 24**. All W1–W5.

### Component 3 alternative (only if that is the route)
Three assignments of ~500–800 words: (1) discuss/argue/persuade in response to a text or texts,
(2) describe, (3) narrate. **65 writing marks + 15 reading marks** (reading tested in Assignment 1
only). First draft of one assignment must be included; the text(s) for Assignment 1 must be attached.
Dictionaries **may** be used. Level descriptions are in the syllabus itself (Tables A and B, p.25–27).
