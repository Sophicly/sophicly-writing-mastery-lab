## C. Corrections & regimes (§9.3 + §11.1)

**C1 — Add the THIRD regime, with precedence.**
REC: **WRONG → FAILED → WEAK/RESOLVED** (evaluated in that order). **✅ CONFIRMED (Neil 2026-07-18) — took the recommendation.**

**C2 — What counts as WRONG?**
REC: **Only a VERIFIABLE error** — misread of the text · false context/biographical fact · misidentified technique. NOT a weak-but-valid interpretation (→ one-push) or empty/off-track (→ ladder). Form = 3 parts (name the error · why · the fix), wise-framing, **FREE, no rung climb.** Interpretation caveat: correct the **grounding**, never impose your reading. **✅ CONFIRMED (Neil 2026-07-18) — took the recommendation.**

**C3 — Redefine "off-track"/failed.**
REC: **failed = non-engagement (drift), NOT "incorrect."** An incorrect answer is `wrong` (falsifiable) or `weak` (interpretive) — never `failed`. Why: prevents the whole ladder mis-firing on a wrong-but-engaged answer. **✅ CONFIRMED (Neil 2026-07-18) — took the recommendation.**
