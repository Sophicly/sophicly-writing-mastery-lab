## Q27.1
**Marks:** 24
**AOs:** AO1,AO2
### Question
In ‘The washing never gets done’, how does the poet present ideas about daily life and connecting with the natural world?

### Poem
**Title:** The washing never gets done
**Poet:** Jaan Kaplinski

1  The washing never gets done.
2  The furnace never gets heated.
3  Books never get read.
4  Life is never completed.
5  Life is like a ball which one must continually
6  catch and hit so it won't fall.
7  When the fence is repaired at one end,
8  it collapses at the other. The roof leaks,
9  the kitchen door won't close, there are cracks in the foundation,
10 the torn knees of children's pants…
11 One can't keep everything in mind. The wonder is
12 that beside all this one can notice
13 the spring which is so full of everything
14 continuing in all directions – into the evening clouds,
15 into the redwing's* song and into every
16 drop of dew on every blade of grass in the meadow,
17 as far as the eye can see, into the dusk.
*redwing: a type of bird

## Q27.2
**Marks:** 8
**AOs:** AO2
### Question
In both ‘A Day in Autumn’ and ‘The washing never gets done’, the poets present ideas about daily life and connecting with the natural world. What are the similarities and/or differences between the methods the poets use to present these ideas?

### Poem
**Title:** A Day in Autumn
**Poet:** R S Thomas

1  It will not always be like this,
2  The air windless, a few last
3  Leaves adding their decoration
4  To the trees’ shoulders, braiding the cuffs
5  Of the boughs with gold; a bird preening

6  In the lawn’s mirror. Having looked up
7  From the day’s chores, pause a minute,
8  Let the mind take its photograph
9  Of the bright scene, something to wear
10 Against the heart in the long cold.
