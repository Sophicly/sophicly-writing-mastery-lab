## Source A
**Title:** An Unknown Girl
**Author:** Moniza Alvi
**Context:** Remind yourself of An Unknown Girl, taken from the Pearson Edexcel International GCSE English Anthology.

1  In the evening bazaar
2  studded with neon
3  an unknown girl
4  is hennaing¹ my hand.
5  She squeezes a wet brown line
6  from a nozzle.
7  She is icing my hand,
8  which she steadies with hers
9  on her satin-peach knee.
10 In the evening bazaar
11 for a few rupees
12 an unknown girl
13 is hennaing my hand.
14 As a little air catches
15 my shadow-stitched kameez²
16 a peacock spreads its lines
17 across my palm.
18 Colours leave the street
19 float up in balloons.
20 Dummies in shop-fronts
21 tilt and stare
22 with their Western perms.
23 Banners for Miss India 1993,
24 for curtain cloth
25 and sofa cloth
26 canopy me.
27 I have new brown veins.
28 In the evening bazaar
29 very deftly
30 an unknown girl
31 is hennaing my hand.
32 I am clinging
33 to these firm peacock lines
34 like people who cling
35 to the sides of a train.
36 Now the furious streets
37 are hushed.
38 I’ll scrape off

39 the dry brown lines
40 before I sleep,
41 reveal soft as a snail trail
42 the amber bird beneath.
43 It will fade in a week.
44 When India appears and reappears
45 I’ll lean across a country
46 with my hands outstretched
47 longing for the unknown girl
48 in the neon bazaar.

Answer the question in this section.

Remind yourself of An Unknown Girl, taken from the Pearson Edexcel International GCSE English Anthology.

How does the writer try to present strong feelings in An Unknown Girl?

In your answer, you should write about:

- how the writer feels about the experience with the girl

- how the writer feels about the culture of India

- the use of language and structure.

You should support your answer with close reference to the poem, including brief quotations.
