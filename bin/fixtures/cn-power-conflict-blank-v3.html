Your living reference for this anthology, built in two halves. The quiz fills the Poetic Forms organiser as you master each form — what it is, its features, its effects, and how form shapes meaning. Then, in Conceptual Notes, you and Sophia study the anthology poem by poem — speaker, context, form, language, themes, purpose, and the big message, each anchored to key quotes.
